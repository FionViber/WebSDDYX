Missing-glyph compatibility fonts

Modified 2026-10-28: subset to audited SDDYX missing Unicode glyphs, renamed family, and converted to WOFF. Glyph outlines retained. This compatibility font is not an original game font.

Original game font files are unchanged. The extra families contain only the audited missing codepoints and are appended after every original fallback. They provide legible fallback, not a claim to match an absent original glyph.
